Who we are

About DOT Olympiads

An academic competition platform designed to strengthen conceptual understanding, reasoning, application and academic growth.

◎

Our Vision

To build a nationally respected Olympiad platform that empowers every learner to discover their potential, strengthen conceptual understanding, and develop the reasoning, analytical thinking and problem-solving abilities needed to excel in an evolving world.

✦

Our Mission

To make meaningful academic competition accessible through structured, curriculum-aligned assessments that encourage conceptual clarity, independent thinking, application of knowledge and continuous academic growth across Classes 1–10.

⌘

Our Objectives

Strengthen conceptual understanding, develop reasoning and application, recognise academic talent, provide structured evaluation, encourage healthy competition and support institutions with transparent assessment.

Fairness · Integrity · Clarity

Examination Rules

▤

Exam Format

Classes 1–10, institution-led participation, objective MCQs on OMR sheets, five options A–E, one correct answer and 60 minutes.

✓

OMR & Marking

Correct answer earns one mark. Wrong, blank or multiple-marked answers earn zero. No negative marking applies.

⚑

Integrity & Conduct

Prohibited devices are not permitted unless authorised. Malpractice may result in cancellation, disqualification or withheld awards. Absent students receive no score and no automatic re-exam.

Transparent assessment

Evaluation & Ranking

Marks, percentage and applicable ranks are determined after OMR validation and result verification.

Scoring
  • Correct answer = 1 mark
  • Wrong, blank or multiple-marked answer = 0
  • Percentage = marks obtained ÷ maximum marks × 100
  • Evaluation: OMR validation → answer matching → scoring → result verification → final result
Ranking & Tie-breaks
  • National Rank
  • State Rank
  • Institution Rank
  • Class Rank
  • Tie-break: Reasoning → DOT Challenge → Application → shared rank if still tied
  • No speed/time-based tie-break.

Need to know

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in DOT Olympiads?

Students in Classes 1–10 can participate through registered schools and academic academies. Individual and parent-direct registration is not available.

Which subjects are available?

Mathematics, Science, English, Social Studies, Computer Science and General Knowledge.

What is the examination mode?

An institution-based offline OMR examination with objective multiple-choice questions and five options (A–E).

Is there negative marking?

No. Correct answers receive one mark; incorrect, blank or multiple-marked answers receive zero.

Can a student choose more than one subject?

Yes. A student may choose one, multiple or all six subjects through the registered institution.

Are learning resources available now?

Resources will show Coming Soon until the relevant materials are prepared and published.

When are awards and certificates finalised?

Award eligibility and merit thresholds must be defined and published before the examination.
